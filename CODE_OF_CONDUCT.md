# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

This project welcomes respectful participation regardless of background, identity,
experience, or ability. This policy applies to project issues, pull requests,
reviews, discussions, and communication where someone represents the project.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code with care; offer specific, constructive feedback.
- Respect different perspectives, skill levels, names, and pronouns.
- Accept corrections and take responsibility for the effects of your words.
- Respect privacy and give credit for other people's work.

Harassment, discriminatory remarks, sexualized attention, threats, personal
attacks, deliberate disruption, and sharing private information without consent
are unacceptable.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to the maintainer, `mhasoneh`, through an existing
private contact channel. No dedicated conduct-reporting address is configured yet.
If you do not have a private contact channel, you may open an issue asking only
for a private way to contact the maintainer. Do not include names, incident
details, or other personal information in that public request.

Provide relevant context, links, and your preferred way to receive a response.
Reports will be handled with discretion; information may need to be shared with
those necessary to investigate and respond. Confidentiality cannot be guaranteed.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove harmful content, request changes in behavior,
issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or ban repeated or severe
misconduct. Responses should consider severity, impact, context, and prior conduct.
Retaliation against a reporter or participant in an investigation is prohibited.

A participant may privately request reconsideration with additional context.
No fixed response time or independent appeals body is promised for this personal
educational project.
